# Workspace Rules: Vertikal 2026 Data Analytics (Anti-AI Slop)

Permanent constraints governing all output, design, and code:

1. **Zero Emojis in UI**: Never render emojis in buttons, headers, cards, or titles. Use pure, crisp inline SVGs.
2. **Luminous Bright Yellow & Electric Blue Palette**:
   - Pure, bright, high-contrast visual styling. No murky, dull grays or cliché purple gradients.
   - Bright Yellow (`#FACC15`, `#F59E0B`, `#FEF08A`) and Bright Electric Blue (`#2563EB`, `#3B82F6`, `#0284C7`, `#EFF6FF`).
3. **Brand Identity**:
   - Include SMA Muhammadiyah 10 Gresik logo (`assets/Logo Smam10.jpeg`) in the top app header.
4. **Figma Dashboard Fidelity**:
   - Modeled directly after Figma Chart Components, Sales Analytics, and Brazilian Trade Balance dashboards.
   - 1px hairline borders (`#E2E8F0`), 8px/12px radius, zero blurry drop shadows.
   - High data density, tabular numerals, interactive charts, and sortable tables.
5. **Preserved Features**:
   - Google Sheets live synchronization with editable URL input, auto-refresh, local file import, and export.
   - Light and dark theme toggle.
